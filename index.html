    每一期都这样做出来Every episode is made the same way

    1. 选题卡Topic card
    2. 文案与分镜Script and storyboard
    3. 15 秒开头试片15-second opening test
    4. 完整版Full cut
    5. 发布复盘Post-release review

    为什么先做 15 秒试片？Why test the first 15 seconds first? 复盘发现，大多数人只看前 10 秒左右，开头决定了一条视频能被推给多少人。所以先把开头做对，再做全片。Reviews showed most viewers watch only about the first 10 seconds, and the opening decides how widely a video gets shown. So the opening comes first, and the full video only once it works.

    ↻每期复盘的发现，验证两三期后写回规范，下一期的起点就更高。What each review finds is tested over two or three more episodes, then written back into the playbook, so the next episode starts higher.
